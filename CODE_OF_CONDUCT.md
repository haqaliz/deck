# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Deck to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever
your experience, background, or identity. Deck is a small desktop app; the bar
for participating should stay low, and the tone should stay kind.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a feature and say why.
- Redact personal data (calendar entries, work items, tokens, hostnames) from
  screenshots and logs before posting them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report problems by opening a GitHub issue, or privately to the maintainer via
the email on the commit history. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may block repeat offenders from the repository.
